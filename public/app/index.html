    Questions people ask

    How is my price decided?

    It depends on your model, storage and the condition you tell us about in a few quick questions. You see your exact offer before you book, and the agent checks the same answers at your door.

    Can the price change at pickup?

    Only if the device is different from your answers. The agent marks which answer changed, and the new price comes from the same price list you used, so you see exactly why.

    Are there any fees?

    No. The quote, pickup, phone test and data wipe are free, and nothing is taken out of your payment.

    How fast do I get paid?

    At pickup, as soon as the check passes. You choose cash in your hand or UPI when you book.

    What happens to my data?

    Every device is erased with certified wiping software. The wipe date is on your sale receipt, which you can download from your order page. Back up first, and sign out of iCloud (Find My) or your Google account so the device isn't locked.

    What do I need at pickup?

    The device, a government photo ID such as Aadhaar, PAN, a driving licence or passport, and the purchase bill if the device is under 3 months old.

    Can I sell without the bill or box?

    Yes. The box and charger add a little to your price. The bill is needed only if the device is under 3 months old.

    My phone isn't listed. Can I still sell it?

    Yes. Tap "Don't see your device?" under the models, tell us the model, and we'll call or WhatsApp you with a price.

    I got a higher quote somewhere else

    Tap “Got a higher quote somewhere else?” on your offer screen and add a screenshot of it. We'll check it and call you back. We can't always match it, but we'll try.

    Is the phone test safe?

    Yes. The tests run only inside your browser. The camera and microphone are used live during the test and nothing is recorded or uploaded. Only the pass or fail results are saved with your booking.

    Do you buy broken or dead devices?

    Yes. Choose "Display Issues", "Cracked or Broken Screen" or "Poor" in the quote. If a device can't be reused or repaired, we recycle it for free and send you a recycling certificate.
